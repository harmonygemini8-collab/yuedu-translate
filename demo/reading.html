READING NOTES / 01

Read with context

Context helps us understand a word. The same word can have different meanings in different sentences.

When reading an article, keep the original text in view. Compare important details with the translation before you share them.

A good reading habit starts small. Read one paragraph, look up an unfamiliar word, and continue at your own pace.
